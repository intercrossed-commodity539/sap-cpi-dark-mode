SAP CPI Dark Mode

What it does

A focused set of controls that make SAP CPI comfortable to work in all day.

Comfortable charcoal

A soft dark surface that avoids a harsh pure black and keeps text easy to read.

Presets and fine tuning

Pick Soft, Comfort, or Deep, then adjust brightness, contrast, warmth, and saturation.

Less eye strain

The warmth control lowers blue light for later sessions without washing out colors.

Private by design

No tracking and no network calls. Your settings stay in your browser.

SAP CPI only

The theme applies to SAP Integration Suite pages. Other sites are never touched.

Quick toggle

Turn the theme on or off with a keyboard shortcut, the default is Alt plus Shift plus D.

Install in a minute

Load it as an unpacked extension in Chrome or Edge.

Download the package. Get sap-cpi-dark-mode-v1.3.1.zip and unzip it to a folder you will keep.
Open the extensions page. Go to chrome://extensions in Chrome, or edge://extensions in Edge.
Turn on Developer mode. Use the switch in the top corner of the extensions page.
Load unpacked. Click Load unpacked and select the folder you unzipped.
Open SAP CPI. Use the toolbar icon to toggle the theme, choose a preset, or fine tune.

Keyboard shortcut

Alt+Shift+D

Turns the theme on or off. To change it, open the shortcut steps or set your own at the browser shortcuts page. The popup shows the shortcut that is currently active.

Permissions

  • storage. Saves your settings and syncs them with your browser profile.
  • tabs. Lets the popup confirm the current tab is a SAP CPI page. It does not read page content.
  • Host access to SAP hosts. Needed to apply the theme on SAP CPI pages.

Privacy

This extension does not collect, send, or sell any data. There is no analytics and no remote code. Everything runs locally in your browser.
